AiWong Fitness

AiWong Fitness is a personal, single-user health dashboard operated by Victor Wong. It reads the operator's own wearable data (Fitbit Air via the Google Health API, and WHOOP) and merges it into a private training log that drives daily training, recovery and nutrition check-ins.

It is not a public service. There are no accounts to create, nothing to download, and no data from anyone other than the operator is collected or processed.
